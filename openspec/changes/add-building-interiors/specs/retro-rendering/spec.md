# Spec Delta: retro-rendering

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Civilian floors are furnished and lived in
Civilian floors SHALL be drawn with furniture of their kind, walls and light in the kind's colours, and the people who
are there in their poses.

#### Scenario: Civilian floors are furnished and lived in
- **WHEN** one floor of every kind is rendered from just inside the front door
- **THEN** the view is lit and not empty, and every person of the floor is drawn
