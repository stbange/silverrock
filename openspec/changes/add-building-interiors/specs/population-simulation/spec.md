# Spec Delta: population-simulation

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: People inside take cover
People on a civilian floor SHALL stay at their places in their poses, and SHALL duck or cover their heads once shots
are fired inside or an alarm sounds.

#### Scenario: People inside take cover
- **WHEN** the player fires a weapon inside a bar
- **THEN** the floor panics and the people are drawn taking cover instead of in their earlier poses
