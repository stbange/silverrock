# Spec Delta: news-media

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Headlines without an owner
Headlines about a site with no known owner SHALL say what happened and where without a placeholder for the owner.

#### Scenario: Headlines without an owner
- **WHEN** sabotage, theft, a break-in or a failed raid happens at a site nobody owns
- **THEN** the headline starts with a capital letter, fits 63 characters and does not contain "a firm"
